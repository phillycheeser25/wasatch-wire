PROVO — **BYU beat Arizona 35–13**, leaning on Bear Bachmeier’s legs early and LJ Martin’s finishing work late to move to **2–0, 1–0 in the Big 12**.

Bachmeier ran for touchdowns of one and 19 yards and threw an eight-yard score to Tiger Bachmeier. He finished 19 of 36 for 199 yards, one passing touchdown and no interceptions, adding 65 yards on 12 carries.

Arizona cut the lead to 21–13 in the fourth quarter. Martin answered with touchdown runs of 10 and two yards in the final 3:11, closing a night of **109 yards and two scores on 18 carries**. BYU held the Wildcats to one touchdown and gets Utah State next.
