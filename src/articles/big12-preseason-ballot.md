The easiest preseason column to write would say the Big 12 is unpredictable and leave it there. That would be true enough. It would also dodge the assignment.

So here is the Wasatch Wire pick: **BYU wins the league. Texas Tech reaches the championship game. Utah finishes third and spends November making the top two nervous.**

This is our preseason vibes ballot. It is an editorial prediction, not the current standings, an official poll or a model. The actual conference race has not started. Utah’s Week 0 nonconference win does not count toward it, and this ballot stays a preseason snapshot as results arrive.

## Why BYU gets the first line

The case starts with an offensive partnership we want to watch. LJ Martin gives the Cougars a clear player to organize around. Bear Bachmeier offers the possibility of hurting a defense with both his arm and his feet. We like the combination of a steady point of contact and room for something more ambitious.

BYU also begins at No. 12 in the dynasty media poll. That does not settle the conference race. It does tell us the Cougars enter this world with expectations rather than having to manufacture them.

The schedule offers a real test of the pick: Notre Dame visits in Week 7, Arizona State follows in Week 9, and the Holy War is on the road in Week 10. We are choosing a team we think can survive the stress, not pretending the stress is absent.

## Utah is the pick that could embarrass us

Putting Utah third feels uncomfortable for exactly the right reason. Dampier gives the Utes a quarterback who can keep an offense alive in more than one way. A team with that kind of answer can turn a narrow game into the result that changes the table.

Our hesitation is the late travel. Utah goes to Arizona in Week 11 and TCU in Week 12, immediately after hosting BYU. That is a lot of opportunity for an excellent season to drop one game too many.

We have Utah and Texas Tech at the same projected conference record. Their order is our editorial preference, not a claim that we have solved a future tiebreaker. If the Utes turn the ballot upside down, the column will have earned its place in the archive.

## The contenders behind them

Kansas State is our steadiness pick. Arizona State is our dangerous-night pick. TCU and Iowa State sit in the part of the league where a good team can feel like a title contender on Saturday and a middle-table team a week later.

Houston deserves a place in the argument, too. The game’s poll starts the Cougars at No. 21. We are giving them five conference wins and asking them to show us how much further the story goes.

The bottom half is where confidence gets thinner. We can imagine an upset for every team there. We are less convinced that each can stack the ordinary wins around it. Oklahoma State gets our last-place pick, which is an invitation for the season to make us look foolish.

## Circle November 7

Week 10. BYU at Utah. Rice-Eccles Stadium.

The Holy War is already big enough without forecasting stakes we have not earned. But our ballot makes it a meeting between the projected champion and the team closest to forcing its way into the championship pair. That is a useful place for a rivalry to begin.
