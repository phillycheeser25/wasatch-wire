PROVO — Luke Heller nearly took the opening kickoff all the way. According to Tyler’s game notes, he ran out of gas before reaching the end zone. BYU’s offense would spend much of the night making that feel like a theme.

The Cougars started fast, stalled through two scoreless quarters, then finished with enough separation to make the final look cleaner than the evening: **BYU 27, Wyoming 6**.

Bear Bachmeier completed 24 of 36 passes for 236 yards, three touchdowns and one interception. His last touchdown was the release valve: a floater to LJ Martin, recorded as a 31-yard score with 4:13 left, that pushed the lead to 24–6.

## A quick start, then a long wait

Bachmeier found Martin for an eight-yard touchdown at 14:06 of the first quarter and Jojo Phillips for a three-yard score at 5:07. BYU had a 14–0 lead before the opening period ended.

Then the points stopped. Wyoming stayed within reach with third-quarter field goals from 47 and 45 yards by Erik Sandvik, trimming the margin to 14–6. Chip Stewart’s 31-yard field goal with 8:55 remaining finally got BYU scoring again. Martin’s second touchdown catch settled the game; Stewart added a 40-yarder to finish it.

## The defense kept the night manageable

Wyoming ran 40 times for just 56 yards. Quarterback Tyler Hughes threw for 230 yards on 20-of-36 passing, but the Cowboys never reached the end zone. That gave BYU time to find its finishing stretch without surrendering the lead.

The Cougars’ own ground game left room for improvement: 105 yards on 36 attempts, with Martin carrying 16 times for 60. BYU finished with 341 total yards to Wyoming’s 286. The two touchdown catches made Martin decisive even on a night when the rushing lanes produced little breathing room.

Tyler’s Cougars are **1–0**, joining Utah with an opening win. Arizona visits in Week 2. BYU leaves this one with a defense that held firm, a quarterback who delivered the closing play, and an offense that will want fewer empty stretches next Saturday.
