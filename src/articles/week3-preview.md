BYU enters Week 3 at **No. 8**, Utah at **No. 13**. Both are unbeaten, but their home assignments offer different tests.

**BYU vs. Utah State — Saturday, Sept. 19, 9 p.m. ET.** The Cougars are 2–0 after beating Arizona 35–13. LJ Martin’s 109-yard performance gave the ground game a lift; Bear Bachmeier has 435 passing yards, four passing touchdowns and two rushing scores through two starts. Utah State arrives 1–2 after a 35–21 loss at Washington. BYU’s task: keep the run game rolling and make the passing attack more consistent after Bachmeier’s 19-of-36 night against Arizona.

**Utah vs. Virginia Tech — Saturday, Sept. 19, 3:30 p.m. ET.** Steve Smith Jr.’s Utes enter 2–0 after the 35–19 win at Boise State. Braden Pegan’s 204 receiving yards powered that victory. Virginia Tech offers a chance to sustain the Dampier–Pegan connection before Utah opens Big 12 play at Iowa State in Week 4.

## The recruiting watch

**BYU is fourth for two national top-20 defenders:** No. 14 **Denzel Tiffin**, a linebacker from Mountain View, California, and No. 18 **Patrick Cargile**, a corner from West Linn, Oregon. Oregon leads for Tiffin; Ohio State leads for Cargile. The Cougars have offered both. BYU is also **second behind Texas A&M for No. 32 Moses Smith**, an athlete from Frisco, Texas, with an offer on the table.

**Utah leads for No. 42 Devante Harris**, a 6-foot-4, 238-pound linebacker from Greenbrae, California. Illinois and Oregon are next, and the Utes have offered. Utah also remains first on **No. 35 Harvey Humphries’** visible interest listing. His previously reported Utah–BYU race remains one to follow; the latest board does not show his full school order.

Nationally, Texas has taken No. 1 after beating Ohio State 28–20, and Arch Manning now leads the Heisman watch. The Big 12’s marquee Week 3 matchup is **No. 15 Houston at No. 12 Texas Tech**, Friday at 8 p.m. ET.
