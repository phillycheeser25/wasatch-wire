PROVO — **Bear Bachmeier threw five touchdown passes and BYU beat Utah State 57–21**, moving the Cougars to **3–0** with their most productive offensive performance of the young season.

Bachmeier completed **24 of 33 passes for 330 yards without an interception**. BYU added 213 rushing yards and two touchdowns on 37 carries, finishing with **543 yards on 70 plays**.

The Cougars led 21–7 after one quarter and 34–21 at halftime. They then shut out the Aggies in the second half, adding 13 points in the third quarter and 10 in the fourth. Utah State finished with 307 yards on 77 plays; BYU held an 18–13 advantage in first downs.

Through three starts, Bachmeier has **765 passing yards, nine touchdown passes and one interception**. BYU remains **1–0 in Big 12 play** and heads into its Week 4 bye before visiting TCU in Week 5.
