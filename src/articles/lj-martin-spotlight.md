PROVO — The most reassuring thing about a running back is not the highlight. It is the possibility that, when an offense needs an ordinary answer, he can give it one.

That is where Wasatch Wire’s preseason case for LJ Martin starts. No borrowed rushing total, no assumed award campaign. Just a player whose background makes him an appealing center of gravity for this BYU team.

## The road from El Paso

Martin comes from El Paso, Texas, where he attended Canutillo High School. As a junior he rushed for 2,737 yards and 29 touchdowns; as a senior he added 2,137 yards and 23 scores. He also played basketball and participated in track.

His family has its own athletic roots. His father played basketball at Western New Mexico, his mother played softball there, and an uncle played football at New Mexico. BYU’s biography also notes that Martin enjoys Madden. The back we will follow on a controller happens to enjoy the game on one himself.

## The value of a dependable touch

Martin is the reason we like BYU’s offensive floor. That is an opinion about the coming dynasty, not a claim about a game already played.

A productive running game would let Bear Bachmeier choose his moments. It could keep a close game from becoming a sequence of obvious passing downs and let the Cougars recover their rhythm after a possession goes sideways. Those benefits do not always make a highlight package. They can decide who is still standing in November.

We will watch how Martin’s touches develop: not just the long runs, but his involvement when BYU needs to close a game or get out of an uncomfortable series. The opener against Wyoming gives us the first actual evidence.

## A watch list with room to earn it

Martin is on our Wasatch Heisman radar. He is **not** being inserted into the game’s national Heisman order, which currently lists four other players. The distinction matters: a local player worth following is not automatically a national frontrunner.

If the Cougars turn a preseason promise into a conference-title push, their running back could become central to that story. For now, the campaign begins where it should: with the next carry.
