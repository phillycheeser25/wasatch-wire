PROVO — A quarterback’s last name can arrive before he does. Bear Bachmeier knows a little about that.

His older brother Hank played quarterback at Boise State, Louisiana Tech and Wake Forest. Another brother, Tiger, played receiver at Stanford before moving to BYU. Bear took his own route through Stanford’s spring program and then joined the Cougars. The connections are easy to spot. The job behind center still belongs to him alone.

## A player with more than one gear

Bachmeier grew up in Murrieta, California, and played at Murrieta Valley. A four-star recruit, he accumulated 6,810 passing yards and 59 passing touchdowns in high school, alongside 1,724 rushing yards and 33 rushing scores. Football was not his only sport: he also lettered in baseball and basketball.

Those are background credentials, not numbers we are carrying into this season. His dynasty ledger is empty. The useful question is what that background makes possible once the games begin.

Our read: the extra running threat can change the arithmetic of an ordinary down. A defense that spends a player accounting for the quarterback has one fewer answer elsewhere. Pair that possibility with LJ Martin and BYU has a compelling starting point for an offense.

## Brothers, then teammates

Having Tiger nearby gives the BYU story a family connection that needs no invented locker-room speech. There is a real relationship underneath the jersey numbers. How often it turns into a productive connection in this dynasty is something the season will tell us.

Away from football, Bear plays guitar and studies business management. It is a small detail, but a welcome one: quarterbacks are people before they become a weekly completion percentage.

## Where the dynasty begins

No. 12 BYU opens against Wyoming in Week 1. Bachmeier’s first assignment is to make the offense look settled, give Martin room to work and produce a game we can judge on its own terms.

The big November questions are already there. Notre Dame visits in Week 7. Utah waits at Rice-Eccles in Week 10. Those games can frame a season, but they cannot be played in advance.

For now, the name on the jersey comes with a football family. The results next to it are still his to write.
