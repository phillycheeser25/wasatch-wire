SALT LAKE CITY — **No. 13 Utah beat Virginia Tech 44–34**, pairing Wayshawn Parker’s explosive running with a timely defensive score to stay unbeaten at **3–0**.

Parker rushed for **118 yards and two touchdowns on 18 carries**, including a 68-yard score as the first half expired. Utah led 24–14 at the break, but Virginia Tech trimmed it to 24–21 entering the fourth.

Braden Pegan’s five-yard touchdown catch gave Utah breathing room. **Elijah Davis then returned an interception 73 yards for a touchdown**, the centerpiece of a 20-point fourth quarter. Larry Simmons added a 19-yard touchdown reception with 1:59 left; the Hokies scored again but never caught up.

Devon Dampier finished **17 of 24 for 194 yards, three touchdowns and one interception**, plus 65 rushing yards. Pegan caught eight passes for 76 yards and two scores. Utah outgained Virginia Tech 395–344 and held the ball for 25:24, though a 97-yard kickoff-return touchdown and 299 passing yards by Ethan Grunkemeyer kept the visitors dangerous.

The Utes head to Iowa State in Week 4. Dampier now has **755 passing yards and nine total touchdowns**; Parker has 344 rushing yards, and Pegan has 26 catches for 361 yards and five touchdowns.
