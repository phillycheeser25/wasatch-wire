BOISE — The blue turf belonged to Braden Pegan. Ten catches, 204 yards and two touchdowns gave Utah its biggest receiving performance of the young season and made the Utes’ first road trip a successful one.

**No. 17 Utah beat Boise State 35–19**, breaking a first-quarter tie with 21 unanswered points in the second. Pegan was named **player of the game**, supplying most of the damage through the air as Devon Dampier accounted for four touchdowns.

## Three touchdowns before the break

Dampier found Pegan for a 28-yard score to open the scoring. Boise State answered with Dylan Riley’s four-yard run, leaving the teams level at 7–7 after the first quarter.

Utah’s response decided the afternoon. Pegan caught a second touchdown, this one from 10 yards. Wayshawn Parker added a 12-yard scoring run, and Dampier punched in a one-yard touchdown with 46 seconds left in the half. A tied game had become a 28–7 Utah lead.

Dampier finished 14 of 19 for 270 yards, three passing touchdowns and one interception. He added 53 yards on 10 carries and the rushing score. His 46-yard touchdown pass to Parker in the third quarter pushed Utah to 35 points and restored the separation after Boise State had scored.

## Pegan takes the spotlight

Pegan accounted for 10 of Utah’s 14 completions and 204 of its 270 passing yards. After an eight-catch opener against Utah State, he now has **18 catches, 285 yards and three touchdowns** through two games.

Parker supplied another balanced performance: 112 rushing yards on 22 carries, plus three catches for 56 yards. His two touchdowns came one on the ground and one through the air. Utah finished with 447 total yards, including 177 rushing.

Boise State managed 300 yards. Maddox Madsen threw for 218 yards and two touchdowns, with the Broncos scoring once in each of the final two quarters. Both two-point attempts failed. Utah’s fourth quarter was scoreless, but the first-half surge had given the visitors enough room.

Steve Smith Jr.’s Utes return home **2–0**, with Virginia Tech next. Dampier’s season line stands at 41-of-50 passing for 561 yards, four passing touchdowns and one interception, alongside 106 rushing yards and two scores. Two games in, Utah has a productive quarterback — and a receiver demanding a larger share of the attention.
